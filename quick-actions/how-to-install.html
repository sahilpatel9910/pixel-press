Desktop Converter

Convert, resize and compress images by right-clicking them in Finder. It works on your Mac, without the internet, and never changes your original files.

Install (about 1 minute)

  1. Double-click each of the three files that end in “.workflow” They're in this folder: Compress to Size…, Convert & Resize… and Convert to JPG.
  2. Click “Install” each time macOS asks “Do you want to install the … quick action?” Click Replace if it asks. System Settings may open afterwards. That's normal, so just close it.
  3. Switch them on Open System Settings → General → Login Items & Extensions. Under Extensions, click Finder and turn on the three Desktop Converter actions.
    Shortcut: right-click any image → Quick Actions → Customise…

Use it

Right-click one image, several images or a folder → Quick Actions, then pick:

Compress to Size…

Shows how big your images are, then makes each one smaller than the size you choose: 2 MB for email, 1 MB, 500 KB, 200 KB for web forms, 100 KB or any size you type.

Convert & Resize…

Choose a format (JPG, PNG, HEIC, PDF, TIFF) and a size. It remembers your last choice, so next time you just press Return twice.

Convert to JPG

One click. Perfect for iPhone photos (HEIC) that won't open on Windows.

New images are saved right next to the originals, for example beach (compressed).jpg beside beach.jpg. Your originals are never changed. A notification shows how much space you saved, and Finder highlights the new file.

If something doesn't work

The actions don't appear in the right-click menu

Make sure they're switched on (step 3). If they still don't appear, log out of your Mac and back in.

macOS shows a security warning

These actions aren't from the App Store, so macOS may ask you to confirm them once. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway, then try again.

macOS asks for access to your Desktop or Documents

Click Allow. The action needs to read the images you picked and save the new ones next to them.

How do I remove it?
  1. In Finder, press ⇧ ⌘ G and go to ~/Library/Services
  2. Move the three Desktop Converter “.workflow” files to the Bin.
